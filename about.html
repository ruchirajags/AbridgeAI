About AbridgeAI

Reading is precious. Don’t waste it.

We believe no one should drown in information. AbridgeAI distills long content into its essence, so you can decide what deserves your full attention.

Our mission

Give readers their time back

Every day, people spend hours wading through newsletters, reports, meeting transcripts and research that could have been summarized in minutes. AbridgeAI started with a simple question: what if the summary came first?

The MVP you can try today is a private, in-browser abridgement engine. Paste anything, and it selects the most information-dense sentences to produce a faithful, readable summary — no uploads, no tracking, no account required.

  • Privacy is a feature, not a policy page
  • Fast beats fancy — summaries in seconds
  • Faithful output that never invents facts

How the MVP works

1. Sentence scoring. Every sentence is scored by how often its important keywords appear across the document.

2. Position bias. Opening and closing sentences get a small bonus because they usually carry the thesis.

3. Selection. The top-scoring sentences are kept in their original order to form a coherent summary.

4. In-browser. All of it runs locally — nothing is sent to a server.
